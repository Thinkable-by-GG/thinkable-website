<h2>Text messaging (SMS) program</h2>
<p><strong>Program name:</strong> Thinkable care messages.</p>
<p><strong>What you receive:</strong> if you provide your mobile number and tick the text-message consent box on one of our forms, or give the same consent to a partner clinic's staff, Thinkable will send you non-marketing text messages about your screening, appointments, check-ins and care reminders relating to the partner clinic you are working with. Messages identify Thinkable and the clinic. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages under this program.</p>
<p><strong>Message frequency:</strong> varies, up to 8 messages per month.</p>
<p><strong>Cost:</strong> message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.</p>
<p><strong>Opt out:</strong> reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You can also ask the clinic or email <a href="mailto:info@thinkable.app">info@thinkable.app</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Help:</strong> reply HELP to any message, or email <a href="mailto:info@thinkable.app">info@thinkable.app</a>.</p>
