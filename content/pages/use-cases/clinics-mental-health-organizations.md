<p class="lead">Thinkable can help clinics and mental health organizations extend education and guided support between appointments while keeping clinical care at the center.</p>
<h2>Support between care touchpoints</h2>
<p>Patients often leave a session with ideas to practice, but need structure to keep that work visible. Thinkable can support psychoeducation, guided exercises, and clearer next steps between care touchpoints.</p>
<ul><li>Between-session education and exercises.</li><li>Support pathways for anxiety, OCD, stress, and related needs.</li><li>Language that positions Thinkable as a support layer, not a replacement for care.</li><li>Resources that help teams explain the support path clearly.</li></ul>
<h2>Where clinics may use Thinkable</h2>
<p>Thinkable can fit around intake, patient education, post-session reinforcement, or program follow-up depending on the clinic workflow.</p>
<ul><li>After assessment or intake.</li><li>Between therapy sessions.</li><li>After a care milestone.</li><li>As a structured resource for patients waiting for formal support.</li></ul>
