<p class="lead">Thinkable supports the full TMS patient lifecycle: helping people find your clinic, helping your team coordinate the next step, and keeping digital support available during and after treatment.</p>
<h2>Two routes into one clinic workflow</h2><p>Direct patient acquisition combines branded pages, TMS education, and screening. Referral development helps your clinic identify local providers, prepare personalized outreach, and build awareness. Your team owns the clinical decisions and provider relationships.</p>
<h2>Keep intake moving</h2><p>Bring screening context, patient stages, tasks, and handoff emails together in the clinic workspace. See what needs attention and keep the next action visible to your team.</p>
<h2>Support patients between visits</h2><p>Digital exercises, check-ins, and assessments give patients ways to stay engaged beyond the appointment. Assessment history and engagement context support conversations with the care team.</p>
<h2>Continue the connection after the course</h2><p>Ongoing digital support and follow-up check-ins help your clinic maintain contact after the acute treatment course. The aim is a continuing relationship around care.</p>
<h2>Start with a focused implementation</h2><ol><li>Map your patient population, service area, and intake workflow.</li><li>Agree on screening criteria, staff responsibilities, and data requirements.</li><li>Configure the agreed workflows and orient your team.</li><li>Evaluate use, patient engagement, and the clinic experience together.</li></ol>
<p>We confirm the scope and availability of each capability before launch. Thinkable is an educational and digital support layer, not an EHR or a replacement for treatment. Screening does not determine clinical eligibility.</p>
