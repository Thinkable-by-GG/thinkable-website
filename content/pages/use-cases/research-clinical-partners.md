<p class="lead">Thinkable can support research and clinical partners that need a structured mental health support layer for pilots, evidence conversations, and program design.</p>
<h2>Built for careful evaluation</h2><p>Research and clinical partnerships often need to define the audience, use case, support claims, and outcome questions before launch. Thinkable gives teams a clearer starting point for that review.</p>
<ul><li>Pilot planning for defined populations or programs.</li><li>Evidence and claims language review.</li><li>Support pathways that can sit around clinical or research workflows.</li><li>Clearer stakeholder discussion before implementation.</li></ul>
<h2>Useful evaluation questions</h2><ul><li>Who is the support layer for?</li><li>Where does Thinkable appear in the participant, patient, or member journey?</li><li>What outcomes, engagement signals, or implementation questions matter?</li><li>What language must be reviewed before rollout?</li></ul>
