<p class="lead">Thinkable gives device and treatment partners a way to support the patient and clinic experience around treatment, from first inquiry through ongoing care.</p>
<h2>Support the clinic beyond the device</h2><p>The platform connects acquisition and provider referral development with clinic coordination and patient support. A shared workflow keeps the next step visible while digital check-ins and exercises extend the experience between visits.</p>
<h2>Start with a defined clinic pilot</h2><p>BrainsWay sponsors our current TMS clinic pilot. The pilot is an opportunity to evaluate the clinic experience, patient engagement, and workflow fit. It is not a claim of proven treatment or commercial outcomes.</p>
<h2>What a partnership can cover</h2><ul><li>Patient education and screening journeys for clinic partners.</li><li>Provider referral development and outreach preparation.</li><li>Intake coordination and patient handoffs.</li><li>Digital support during and after treatment.</li><li>A shared evaluation plan based on the agreed implementation.</li></ul>
<h2>Define success together</h2><p>We align on the clinic population, roles, implementation scope, review requirements, and measures of success. Reporting requirements are agreed during scoping; custom sponsor reports and integrations are not assumed to be available.</p>
<p>Thinkable’s digital support and its research base are distinct from device efficacy claims. Clinical treatment decisions remain with the provider.</p>
