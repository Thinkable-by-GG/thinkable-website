<p class="lead">Research and clinical partners need a clear way to evaluate program fit, evidence language, study goals, and implementation boundaries. Thinkable can support a structured conversation around pilots, clinical collaboration, and research-informed deployment.</p><h2>Where collaboration may fit</h2><ul><li>Pilot programs that need guided support content around defined user groups.</li><li>Clinical collaborations that require reviewable language and clear boundaries.</li><li>Research pathways where outcomes, measures, and participant experience must be planned early.</li><li>Evidence reviews for partners assessing whether Thinkable belongs in a care or product context.</li></ul><h2>Research review focus</h2><p>Bring the target population, proposed study or program context, review requirements, success measures, and any institutional constraints.
