<p class="lead">Medical device and treatment partners often need support around patient understanding, preparation, follow-through, and emotional barriers. Thinkable can help teams review where structured mental health support may fit around a treatment journey.</p><h2>Where support may be useful</h2><ul><li>Before treatment, when patients need education and expectation-setting.</li><li>During treatment, when consistency, reassurance, and guided practice matter.</li><li>After treatment milestones, when patients need support staying engaged with recommended next steps.</li><li>Across care-team communication, when support language must stay clear and responsible.</li></ul><h2>What partners clarify</h2><p>A medical-device partnership defines the patient journey, approved claims environment, review ownership, escalation language, and what content can responsibly support patient education. Thinkable is reviewed as a structured support layer, not as a replacement for clinical care or device instructions.</p><h2>Related pages</h2><ul><li><a href="/use-cases/medical-device-treatment-partners/">Medical device treatment partners</a></li><li><a href="/resources/medical-device-support-layer/">Mental health support layers for medical device companies</a></li><li><a href="/resources/patient-engagement-after-treatment/">Patient engagement after treatment touchpoints</a></li></ul><p><a class="button" href="/partner-demo/">Discuss treatment support</a></p>
