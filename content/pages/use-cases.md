<p class="lead">Thinkable gives partner organizations a structured support layer for mental health education, guided exercises, and audience-specific care pathways.</p>
<h2>Partner settings Thinkable supports</h2>
<p>The same core platform can be adapted to different partnership settings, from clinical care and digital health products to employee benefits, treatment support, and research programs.</p>
<h2><a href="/use-cases/clinics-mental-health-organizations/">Clinics and mental health organizations</a></h2>
<p>For clinics, Thinkable can support patient education, between-session engagement, and better-fit conversations. It is positioned around clinical support, not as a replacement for care.</p>
<h2><a href="/use-cases/digital-health-partners/">Digital health partners</a></h2>
<p>For digital health teams, Thinkable can add guided mental health support inside an existing product experience. Users receive practical exercises and education while product teams strengthen engagement and support depth.</p>
<h2><a href="/use-cases/medical-device-treatment-partners/">Medical device and treatment partners</a></h2>
<p>For medical device and treatment teams, Thinkable can act as a companion education layer around treatment journeys. It supports follow-up, helps users understand next steps, and gives partner teams a clearer way to discuss support needs.</p>
<h2><a href="/use-cases/eap-employer-mental-health-support/">Employer and EAP programs</a></h2>
<p>For employers and EAP teams, Thinkable can provide a private, structured support option for employees who need mental health education and guided exercises before, between, or alongside other benefits.</p>
<h2><a href="/use-cases/research-clinical-partners/">Research and clinical partners</a></h2>
<p>For research and clinical partners, Thinkable can support pilots, evidence conversations, and program design. This path is built for teams that need careful positioning, review requirements, and a shared understanding of outcomes before launch.</p>
<h2>Where Thinkable fits</h2>
<p>Thinkable is best presented as a partner support layer: structured education, guided exercises, and audience-specific pathways that help organizations support people more clearly without turning the website into a generic app marketing page.</p>
