<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"lead"} -->
<p class="lead">A Thinkable partner demo is a focused conversation about where structured mental health support could fit in your organization and what would need to be true for a partnership to work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Bring the use case</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most useful demo starts with a clear picture of the audience you serve and the support gap you are trying to address.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>The people you want to support: patients, users, employees, members, or study participants.</li>
<li>The setting where support is needed: clinic, product, benefit program, treatment journey, or research project.</li>
<li>The current gap: education, engagement, between-session support, follow-up, or guided exercises.</li>
<li>Any clinical, privacy, compliance, or evidence questions that need review.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>What the demo covers</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The conversation is built around fit. It can focus on the relevant use case, the role Thinkable would play, and the practical steps needed to evaluate partnership.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>How Thinkable could support the audience journey.</li>
<li>Which programs, resources, or support areas are most relevant.</li>
<li>Where evidence and claims language need careful review.</li>
<li>What an implementation path could look like if there is a fit.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>After the conversation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the use case is strong, the next step is usually a more detailed review with the right stakeholders.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>Confirm the audience and support scenario.</li>
<li>Review content, evidence, and messaging requirements.</li>
<li>Map operational needs such as access, routing, reporting, and ownership.</li>
<li>Decide whether to move toward a scoped partnership plan.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
