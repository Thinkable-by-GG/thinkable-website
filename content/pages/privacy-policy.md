<p class="lead">This Privacy Policy explains how Thinkable handles information submitted through this website by visitors and organizations evaluating a potential partnership, and how we handle mobile numbers and consent for the Thinkable text-messaging program.</p>
<h2>Who we are</h2>
<p>This website and the Thinkable platform are operated by GGTUDE LTD, a company registered in Israel, doing business as Thinkable. References to "Thinkable", "we" and "us" mean GGTUDE LTD.</p>
<h2>Information submitted through forms</h2>
<p>When you submit a partner demo or contact form, Thinkable may collect the information you provide, such as your name, work email, organization, role, audience, use case, and message.</p>
<h2>How the information is used</h2>
<p>Form information is used to review partnership fit, respond to inquiries, prepare relevant follow-up, and understand which use cases are most relevant to the organization contacting Thinkable.</p>
<h2>Business and partnership context</h2>
<p>The forms on this website are intended for business and partnership inquiries. Please do not submit confidential patient information, emergency requests, or sensitive clinical details through the website forms.</p>
<h2>Website operation</h2>
<p>The website may use standard hosting, security, spam-prevention, and form-processing tools to operate the site and protect submissions.</p>
<h2>Sharing</h2>
<p>Thinkable does not sell website form submissions. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or respond to partnership inquiries.</p>
<h2>Text messaging (SMS)</h2>
<p>If you give us your mobile number and tick the text-message consent on one of our forms, or give that consent to the staff of a clinic that uses Thinkable, we store your number, the date and time of your consent and your contact preference, and Thinkable may send you non-marketing text messages about your screening, appointments, check-ins and care reminders on behalf of that clinic. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. The full program terms are in our <a href="/terms/">Terms</a>.</p>
<p>Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share your number only with the messaging provider that delivers the texts and with the clinic you are working with. We do not sell phone numbers.</p>
<h2>Contact</h2>
<p>Privacy questions can be sent to <a href="mailto:info@thinkable.app">info@thinkable.app</a>.</p>
