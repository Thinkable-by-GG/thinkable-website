<p class="lead">Mental health support often depends on what happens after a conversation ends. Partners need practical ways to reinforce education, keep people engaged, and make the next step feel manageable.</p><h2>What structured support can include</h2><ul><li>Guided mental health exercises that are easy to revisit.</li><li>Psychoeducation that supports care conversations without replacing care.</li><li>Clear pathways for users who need more help or a partner-provided service.</li></ul><h2>How to evaluate fit</h2><p>Review the audience, the care journey, the intended support moments, and the language needed to introduce the experience responsibly.</p><h2>Related pages</h2><ul><li><a href="/use-cases/clinics-mental-health-organizations/">Clinics and mental health organizations</a></li><li><a href="/resources/therapy-tools-and-psychoeducation/">Therapy tools and psychoeducation</a></li><li><a href="/resources/implementation-guide-partner-support/">Implementation guide for partner support</a></li></ul><p><a class="button" href="/partner-demo/">Request demo</a></p>
