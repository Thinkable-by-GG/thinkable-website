<h2>Evidence shapes responsible positioning</h2><p>Partner teams need to understand what a mental health support solution is designed to do, what it is not designed to do, and what evidence is available for review.</p><h2>Questions to ask</h2><ul><li>Which populations and support needs are most relevant?</li><li>What outcomes, studies, or evidence summaries can be reviewed?</li><li>What claims language should be avoided or clarified?</li></ul><h2>Who should be involved</h2><p>Evidence review often needs input from product, clinical, legal, compliance, research, and partnership stakeholders before launch.</p><p><a class="button" href="/science-evidence/">Review science and evidence</a></p>
