<h2>Start with the care pathway</h2><p>Clinics should evaluate digital support in the context of how patients already move through intake, appointments, education, follow-up, and between-session work.</p><h2>Key questions</h2><ul><li>Which patients or members would use the support?</li><li>When would it be introduced?</li><li>What should users do if they need clinical help?</li></ul><h2>Fit before rollout</h2><p>A partner demo should clarify audience fit, implementation effort, evidence review, and how the clinic wants to explain the support layer.</p><p><a class="button" href="/buyers/clinic-organization-buyers/">View clinic buyer page</a></p>
