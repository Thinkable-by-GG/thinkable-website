<h2>Why the space between touchpoints matters</h2><p>Many mental health journeys depend on what happens after the appointment, coaching session, treatment milestone, or benefits interaction ends. Partners need structured ways to keep support understandable and actionable without pretending that a website or app replaces care.</p><h2>What good between-session support does</h2><ul><li>Reinforces concepts introduced in care, coaching, or education.</li><li>Gives users guided exercises they can return to.</li><li>Keeps escalation and partner support paths clear.</li></ul><h2>Questions for partner review</h2><p>Before adding a support layer, define the audience, the support moment, the partner workflow, the intended language, and what a user should do when they need more help.</p><p><a class="button" href="/partner-demo/">Discuss partner fit</a></p>
