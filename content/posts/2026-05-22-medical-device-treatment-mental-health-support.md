<h2>Treatment journeys include more than the treatment moment</h2><p>Patients may need education, reassurance, and structured support before or after a device-based or treatment-centered experience. That support needs to be positioned carefully and reviewed by the right teams.</p><h2>Useful support moments</h2><ul><li>Before treatment, when patients need orientation.</li><li>After treatment, when follow-up and engagement matter.</li><li>Between touchpoints, when users need practical next steps.</li></ul><h2>Review considerations</h2><p>Medical device partners should review patient context, claims boundaries, evidence, communication language, and how support connects to existing care pathways.</p><p><a class="button" href="/buyers/mental-health-medical-device-companies/">View medical device buyer page</a></p>
